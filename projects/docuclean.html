PDF Scanner & OCR: DocuClean

Scan documents, receipts and ID cards to PDF, then find any page by a word inside. No ads, no watermark.

Download on the App Store

What it does

Scan documents, receipts, letters and ID cards into clean PDFs, then find any of them by a word inside. DocuClean reads every page right on your iPhone: no account, no uploads, no watermark, no ads.

No desk scanner or fax machine needed: scan a signed form, a contract or a bill in seconds and share the PDF by email, Messages or Files.

Free

  • Scan with the camera or import from Photos.
  • On-device text recognition on every page, so you can search your library by title or by any word inside a scan.
  • Export and share clean PDFs, no watermark, no ads.
  • Rename, organize and delete your documents.
  • Keep up to 5 documents.

On-device AI (iOS 27+)

  • AI Naming suggests a title for every scan from its recognized text.
  • Summarize turns a long scan into a short summary you can copy or share.
  • Image Recognition tags each page (receipt, letter, ID, photo) and makes it searchable.
  • Local, private and secure: these AI features run fully on your device, nothing is uploaded, free for everyone, no Pro required.

DocuClean Pro

  • Save unlimited documents.
  • Export password-locked PDFs.
  • Export the recognized text as a plain text file.

Privacy

DocuClean does not collect any data, does not require an account, and never sends your scans anywhere. The camera is used only to capture pages, and every scan, thumbnail, recognized word and AI result stays local, private and secure on your device.

Subscription

DocuClean Pro is an auto-renewing subscription: $4.99 per week, or $49.99 per year with a 3-day free trial. Payment is charged to your Apple ID account at confirmation of purchase. The subscription automatically renews unless it is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period; your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours prior to the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel your subscription anytime in your Apple ID account settings after purchase. Any unused portion of a free trial is forfeited when you purchase a subscription.
