Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Data Collection

We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers.

HealthKit Data

The app accesses HealthKit data with your explicit permission. All health data:

  • Remains on your device
  • Is never transmitted to any server
  • Is used only for local calculations and display
  • Follows Apple's HealthKit privacy requirements

Google Health

If you choose to connect Google Health (for example, to use a Fitbit Air), you sign in with Google and the app requests read-only access to the following data through the Google Health API:

  • Heart rate, resting heart rate, respiratory rate, heart rate variability, and blood oxygen (SpO2), via the googlehealth.health_metrics_and_measurements.readonly scope
  • Sleep sessions and sleep stages, via the googlehealth.sleep.readonly scope
  • Steps, exercise and workouts, and calories, via the googlehealth.activity_and_fitness.readonly scope

This data:

  • Is downloaded directly from Google to your device
  • Is never transmitted to our servers or any third party
  • Is used only to calculate and display your stress level and health metrics, processed entirely on your device
  • Is never used for advertising, sold, or used to train AI models

Your Google sign-in is stored securely in the device Keychain. You can disconnect Google Health at any time in Settings, which revokes the app's access. You can also remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

NoStress's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements, and the Google Health API Developer and User Data Policy.

Data Protection

We protect sensitive data, including health data and Google user data, with these measures:

  • Data from Google travels only between Google and your device over encrypted HTTPS (TLS) connections
  • Google sign-in credentials are kept in the iOS Keychain, encrypted by the operating system
  • Any data the app keeps on your device is encrypted at rest by iOS Data Protection and is readable only by the app
  • We run no servers that receive or store your data, so neither we nor any third party can access it
  • Read-only access: the app never writes to or changes your Google Health data

Local Storage

User preferences are stored locally on your device using standard iOS storage mechanisms.
