Use of Service

The app provides stress monitoring and health insights based on your device's HealthKit data. All health data remains on your device and is not transmitted to any server. You may optionally connect Google Health to add heart rate, resting heart rate, respiratory rate, heart rate variability, blood oxygen (SpO2), sleep sessions and stages, steps, exercise/workouts, and calories from connected devices such as Fitbit Air; that data is read directly from Google to your device.
