Snore Recorder: SleepTrack

Do you snore or talk in your sleep? Free overnight recording, a morning sleep score and a smart alarm. Nothing leaves your iPhone.

Download on the App Store

What it does

Do you snore or talk in your sleep? SleepTrack records it overnight, plays back the clips in the morning with a simple sleep score, and wakes you with a smart alarm. Everything stays on your iPhone.

Free, forever

  • Nightly tracking: records snoring and sleep talk automatically, with an ambient noise reading.
  • A morning report: sleep score, sound recordings grouped by type, and full night details.
  • The last 7 nights and 7-day trend chart in Statistics.
  • A smart alarm with a wake-up window, plus a bedtime reminder.
  • 5 free sleep sounds and a sleep timer.

SleepTrack Pro

  • Full sleep history and trend charts beyond 7 nights.
  • Every sleep sound in the library, with no limit.
  • Save your own custom sound mixes.

Privacy

SleepTrack does not collect any data, does not require an account, and never uploads your recordings. The microphone is used only to detect snoring and sleep talk, and every clip, score and report stays stored on your device.

Subscription

SleepTrack Pro is an auto-renewing subscription: $7.99 per week, or $59.99 per year with a 3-day free trial. Payment is charged to your Apple ID account at confirmation of purchase. The subscription automatically renews unless it is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period; your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours prior to the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel your subscription anytime in your Apple ID account settings after purchase. Any unused portion of a free trial is forfeited when you purchase a subscription.
