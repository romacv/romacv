NoStress

HRV Stress Tracker for iPhone and Apple Watch.

NoStress reads heart rate variability from Apple Watch and Fitbit to show your stress level in real time, on the wrist and on the phone. It sends gentle check-in notifications when your body needs a pause, and turns HRV, resting heart rate, sleep and activity into a daily overview and monthly trends. Built with SwiftUI, HealthKit, WatchKit and WidgetKit.

Connect Apple Health or, optionally, Google Health to see your stress level alongside heart rate, sleep and activity. Health metrics are processed on your device.
