Math Games for 1st Grade With an AI Tutor

Math Panda is a math tutor for kids ages 6–8. A friendly panda guides children through addition, subtraction, multiplication and division, adapting to each child's pace.

What it does

Kids practice core elementary math through interactive games and quizzes while an AI panda automatically adjusts difficulty so lessons stay achievable: never too easy, never overwhelming. Multiplication and division are taught table by table, from the 2× table through the 9× table, and the panda reads tasks and explanations aloud to support early readers. Behind a parental gate, kids can also ask the AI panda a math question directly and get a friendly, step-by-step explanation.

Who it's for

Parents and teachers of children roughly 6–8 years old (around 1st grade) who want a focused, single-subject math app rather than a broad edutainment platform. Progress reports let parents and teachers track skill development over time, and multi-player support works for siblings or small groups.

Key features

  • AI panda tutor with adaptive difficulty
  • Addition, subtraction, multiplication and division practice
  • Multiplication and division tables, lesson by lesson
  • Voice responses for early readers
  • AI Panda Chat for math questions, behind a parental gate
  • Progress reports for parents and teachers
  • Multi-player support for siblings or classrooms

Pricing

Free to download. The A+ PASS subscription unlocks full access at $3.99/month or $9.99 per 6-month semester, billed through the App Store.

How it is built

Built with: SwiftUI, Firebase AI, Firestore, Lottie, Swift Charts. Runs on: iPhone, iPad.

Frequently asked questions

What age is Math Panda designed for?
Roughly ages 6–8, aligned with 1st-grade math: addition, subtraction, and introductory multiplication and division.
How does the AI tutor work?
The app automatically adjusts question difficulty to your child's current skill level, and a parental-gated AI Panda Chat answers math questions with step-by-step explanations.
Is it safe for kids to use?
Yes, there is no chat with strangers, no social features, and no external links without parental approval. A parental gate protects the paywall, subscription management, and the AI chat feature.

Free to download. The A+ PASS subscription is $3.99/month or $9.99 per 6-month semester.

Yes, detailed progress reports let parents and teachers see skill development over time.
