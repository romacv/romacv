On-Device Background Eraser, Scanner & OCR for iPhone

Pixero removes backgrounds, erases objects, scans documents and reads QR codes and text — every operation runs on your phone, with no cloud uploads and no account.

New: private on-device AI reads labels, answers questions about a photo and suggests what to redact. It needs iOS 27 with Apple Intelligence, and nothing is uploaded.

Download on the App Store

What it does

Pixero is a privacy-first photo editor and scanner built on Apple's Vision framework and Neural Engine. One tap gives you a clean background cutout; paint over anything else you want gone and the magic eraser fills it in. Beyond editing, Pixero doubles as a document scanner with perspective correction and multi-page PDF export, an OCR tool that pulls text out of any photo, and a QR/barcode reader with scan history. A built-in redaction tool blurs, pixelates or blacks out faces and text non-destructively.

Who it's for

Anyone who wants Photoshop-style background removal and a document scanner without sending photos to a server or creating an account — freelancers preparing product photos, students scanning notes, and anyone redacting sensitive documents before sharing them.

Key features

  • One-tap background remover and magic eraser
  • Document scanner with edge detection and multi-page PDF export
  • OCR text recognition — copy or share extracted text
  • QR and barcode scanner with history
  • Face and text redaction (blur, pixelate, solid bar)
  • Photo enhancer, resize/crop presets, watermarking
  • Reusable editing presets, synced across devices via iCloud

Pricing

Free to download. Pixero Pro unlocks the full feature set for $4.99/month or $24.99/year, billed through the App Store.

Frequently asked questions

Does Pixero upload my photos to a server?
No. Background removal, OCR, redaction and every other operation run on-device using Apple Vision and the Neural Engine. Pixero has no servers and requires no account.
Can Pixero scan documents and read QR codes?
Yes — a dedicated document scanner with perspective correction and multi-page PDF export, plus a QR/barcode reader with scan history.
How much does Pixero cost?
Free to download. Pixero Pro is $4.99/month or $24.99/year.
Does Pixero redact faces and text in photos?
Yes — auto-detect or manually paint over faces and text with blur, pixelate or a solid black bar. Redaction is non-destructive; the original image stays intact.
Does Pixero work on iPad?
Yes, it's a universal iOS/iPadOS app requiring iOS 17 or later.
