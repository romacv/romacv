What it does

Currency Converter: Best gives you real-time exchange rates for 170+ currencies plus major cryptocurrencies, in a simple, fast interface. Rates are cached automatically, so conversions keep working without an internet connection — useful when traveling and roaming is off. Save your frequently used currencies as favorites, check historical charts to see how a rate has moved over time, and glance at your top pairs from a home screen widget without opening the app.

Who it's for

Travelers converting prices on the go, online shoppers comparing prices across storefronts, and anyone managing international payments or watching a few currency or crypto pairs day to day.

  • Offline mode for conversions without internet
