Wallet Passes, QR & Scanner

Download on the App Store

What it does

Cardmi keeps every loyalty card and your business card on your iPhone, ready when you need them. Scan the barcode on a store card with the camera, and Cardmi shows it full screen at full brightness, so the cashier scans it first time. No account, no ads, no shopping app around your cards. Your business card lives here too. Create it once, show its QR code, and anyone who scans it with the Camera app opens your card in the browser and can save your contact. Share it as a link or a contact file, and scan paper business cards straight into Contacts. Add your loyalty cards and your business card to Apple Wallet as passes, so they sit with the rest of your cards. Moving from Stocard? Scan your plastic cards or type their numbers once, and they are back in your pocket. Free - Scan barcodes and QR codes with the camera, or type the number by hand. - EAN-13, EAN-8, UPC-A, Code 128, Code 39, QR, PDF417 and Aztec barcodes. - Full-screen barcode with the screen brightness raised while it is open. - One business card with a QR code, share link and contact file. - Control Center control and Siri: "Show my card in Cardmi". - Search, colors and notes for each card. - Keep up to 3 loyalty cards. Cardmi Pro - Unlimited loyalty cards and more than one business card. - Apple Wallet passes for loyalty cards and your business card. - Paper business card scanner that reads text into Contacts or into your own card. - Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets that open a card in one tap, including a business card widget. - Your cards on Apple Watch. Private by design Cards are stored only on your iPhone. Cardmi has no account, and the camera is used only to read a barcode or the text on a paper card. A web business card keeps its details inside the link itself. When you add a card to Apple Wallet, its details are sent securely to our pass-signing service, signed in memory and returned; nothing is stored. Subscription Cardmi Pro is an auto-renewing subscription: $2.99 per week, or $19.99 per year with a 3-day free trial. Payment is charged to your Apple ID account at confirmation of purchase. The subscription automatically renews unless it is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period; your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours prior to the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel your subscription anytime in your Apple ID account settings after purchase. Any unused portion of a free trial is forfeited when you purchase a subscription.
