Track Claud Usage on Your Mac and iPhone

Agents Battery shows your Claud session, weekly and per-model weekly limits as a color-coded battery icon that updates automatically, on your Mac and iPhone. It's listed on the App Store as "AI Limits: Agents Battery".

What it does

Agents Battery is a native Mac menu bar app, with an iPhone app, built for developers who use the Claud CLI and want to see how much of their usage limit is left without switching to a terminal. It tracks three periods — the current 5-hour session, the 7-day weekly limit, and per-model weekly limits when available — and turns each into a battery-style progress bar that goes from green to orange to red as you approach the limit, along with a reset timer. iPhone app with Home Screen, Lock Screen and StandBy widgets: in TestFlight now, coming to the App Store.

How it works

The app reads the OAuth token that the Claud CLI already stores in your macOS Keychain after you sign in to it once, then calls the service's own usage API directly with that token. Nothing runs through a third-party server: there is no backend, no analytics SDK, and no data collection by the developer. The iPhone app receives the same credentials via iCloud Keychain sync from the Mac app.

Who it's for

Anyone who works with Claud regularly and wants an always-visible, at-a-glance sense of remaining quota instead of checking usage manually.

Key features

  • Menu bar battery icon for session, weekly and per-model usage
  • Color-coded progress bars with reset timers
  • Auto-refresh, no manual token entry after first setup
  • iPhone app with Home Screen, Lock Screen and StandBy widgets (TestFlight now, coming to the App Store)
  • Local-only: reads your own Keychain token and calls the service's API directly

Pricing

Free on the Mac App Store. Agents Battery Pro is an optional subscription for iPhone and Mac ($1.99 per week, or $19.99 per year with a 3-day free trial): unlimited accounts, run-out and low-battery alerts, per-model meters, a 7-day trend and every widget.

How it is built

Built with: SwiftUI, AppKit, WidgetKit, two shared Swift packages, Keychain. Runs on: Mac menu bar, iPhone (TestFlight).
