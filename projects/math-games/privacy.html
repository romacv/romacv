English (Primary)

1. Who We Are

Math Panda is an educational mathematics app designed for children ages 6–8. References to "we", "us", or "our" mean the operator of Math Panda.

Contact: https://resrom.com/projects/math-games/contact

For all privacy inquiries — including parental requests to review or delete a child's data — please email us at the address above.

2. Scope of This Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what information Math Panda collects, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the rights you and your child have over that information.

Math Panda is an educational app directed at young learners. We take children's privacy seriously and comply with:

  • The Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) (United States)
  • The General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and its provisions for children's data (EU/EEA)
  • UK GDPR and the Age Appropriate Design Code
  • Other applicable children's privacy laws

3. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum information necessary to run the app.

3.1 Information you provide

Data Example Where it is stored
Child's nickname "Alex", "Мишка" Locally on device + Google Firebase
Gameplay progress Lesson completed, coins earned Google Firebase (Firestore)
  • The nickname is chosen freely by the child or parent inside the app. It does not need to be a real name.
  • We do not collect: full name, email address, phone number, home address, date of birth, photos, voice, or any government-issued identifier.

3.2 Information collected automatically

Data Purpose Retained by
Anonymous Firebase user ID Links gameplay progress across sessions Google Firebase
  • The Firebase user ID is generated randomly by Firebase. It is not linked to any real-world identity, Apple ID, or email address.
  • We do not collect: precise location, contacts, microphone input, camera images, HealthKit data, or advertising identifiers (IDFA / ATTrackingManager is not used).

3.3 AI Tutor (parental gate required)

Math Panda includes an optional AI Math Tutor feature powered by Google Gemini. This feature is only activated after a parent or guardian passes a parental gate (a math problem that a young child cannot reasonably solve).

When the AI Tutor is active, the following is sent to Google Gemini:

  • Child's chosen nickname
  • The current math question text
  • The app's locale (language setting)

No other personal information is sent. The parental gate ensures that a parent has consented to this feature before any data reaches Google's AI service.

4. How We Use Information

Personalize the AI Math Tutor Nickname, question text, locale Parental consent (via gate)
Fix crashes and improve stability Crash logs Legitimate interest
Subscription management Subscription receipt (handled by Apple) Contractual necessity

We do not use any data for behavioural advertising, profiling, or sale to third parties.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA + GDPR-K)

This section applies specifically because Math Panda is directed at children under 13.

7.1 No collection without parental consent

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without verifiable parental consent.
  • The AI Tutor feature, which involves sending data to a third-party AI service, is gated behind a parental gate (a multi-step arithmetic problem designed to be solvable only by an adult). The gate must be passed before data is sent.
  • All other data collected (anonymous user ID, gameplay progress) does not identify the child.

7.2 Parental rights

As a parent or legal guardian, you have the right to:

  • Review the information we hold about your child (email us with your child's nickname and we will provide all data associated with that anonymous user).
  • Delete your child's data entirely (see Section 12).
  • Refuse further collection — uninstalling the app stops all data collection immediately.
  • Withdraw consent for the AI Tutor — this can be done by emailing us; we will disable the AI Tutor for that anonymous user ID.

7.3 Identifiers are anonymous

The identifiers we use (Firebase anonymous user ID) cannot be linked to a real name, email, or physical identity by us. We do not have the technical means to identify who the child is from the data alone.

8. Data Retention

Data Retention period
Gameplay progress (Firestore) 24 months from last activity
Nickname 24 months from last activity
Subscription records As required by Apple (typically 7 years for financial records)

When a parental deletion request is received, we will delete all identifiable data within 30 calendar days and confirm deletion by email.

9. International Data Transfers

Google Firebase and Gemini may process data on servers located in the United States or other countries outside your country of residence. These transfers are carried out under appropriate safeguards, including:

  • Google's Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission
  • Google's Firebase Data Processing and Security Terms

10. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you or your child may have the following rights:

  • Access: Request a copy of data we hold.
  • Deletion: Request that we delete all data.
  • Rectification: Ask us to correct inaccurate data (e.g., change the nickname on record).
  • Restriction: Ask us to stop processing while a dispute is resolved.
  • Withdrawal of consent: Withdraw consent for the AI Tutor at any time.
  • Complaint: Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To exercise any right, submit a request via: https://resrom.com/projects/math-games/contact

11. Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this Privacy Policy, we will:

  1. Display a notice screen inside the app at least 30 days before the change takes effect.
  2. Update the "Effective date" at the top of this document.

Continued use of the app after the effective date constitutes acceptance of the updated policy. For changes that require new parental consent (e.g., new data sharing with a third party), we will collect that consent before the change applies.

12. How to Request Deletion

Option 1 — Email: Submit a data deletion request at https://resrom.com/projects/math-games/contact. Include your child's nickname and approximate date of last use. We will delete all associated data within 30 days.

Option 2 — In-app: Open the app → Settings → "Contact Us" → select "Delete my data". This sends a pre-filled deletion request to our support email.
