Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-13

Agents Battery displays Claud and Codx usage limits on Mac and iPhone, with iPad support and widgets. It has no server operated by us. Provider sign-ins and usage requests go directly to the Claud and Codx services; Claud usage numbers can also pass through your iCloud account.

What stays on your device

  • Codx access and refresh tokens stay in the Keychain on the device where you sign in. Disconnecting a Codx account removes its token. Cached usage numbers, history and preferences use local system storage; an App Group shares usage numbers with widgets.

Network access

Claud on Mac: the app reads the sign-in already stored by your Claud CLI in the macOS Keychain, only to request usage and profile data from the Claud service over HTTPS. It never changes, refreshes or copies that sign-in.

Codx on Mac and iPhone: you sign in to your Codx account through its own sign-in page in your browser (browser sign-in on Mac, device-code sign-in on iPhone). The app reads usage limits from the Codx usage service over HTTPS using its own locally stored token and refreshes the sign-in when needed. It does not read your Codx conversations.

iCloud relay: Apple iCloud key-value storage carries Claud usage numbers from your Mac to your iPhone or iPad on the same iCloud account. Tokens never enter iCloud key-value storage or reach the iPhone through this relay. Codx tokens are not synced through iCloud.

No third-party tracking

Agents Battery has no ads, no cross-app tracking and does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA). The app has no separate analytics or crash-reporting SDK.

Subscriptions

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple's StoreKit. We never see your payment details.

Third-party SDKs

  • Claud and Codx services — sign-in and usage requests go directly to their providers, under their own privacy policies and terms
  • Apple App Store — Privacy · Terms
  • Web3Forms — Privacy · Terms

Contact

Questions about this policy: use the contact form. If you write to us through it, your name, email and message are sent through Web3Forms (a form-processing service) to reach us and are used only to answer you; the app itself never sends them.
