About me

Since 2011, I've been building applications for Apple platforms, both independently and as part of teams, including technical leadership and mentoring. I work with a modern native stack: Swift (SwiftUI, UIKit, AppKit, Combine, Concurrency), having started with Objective‑C.

As soon as AI coding agents appeared, I went all in on agentic development. I build my own harnesses around Claude Code, Codex and Gemini: rules, skills, subagents, MCP servers and automations that plan, write, review and ship code, so one engineer delivers like a team.

Attended Apple WWDC in 2018 and 2019, and regularly join local meetups. In my spare time, I ship my own apps - full cycle from idea to release, including design, development, UI/UX, and ASO.

Working with me, you’ll get:

  • – reliability and on-time delivery
  • – professional communication
  • – efficiency and alignment with requirements
